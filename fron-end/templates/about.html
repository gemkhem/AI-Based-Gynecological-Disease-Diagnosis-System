    Clinical Background

    About the GDDES Healthcare Platform

    Bridging machine learning diagnostics with clinical patient experiences to elevate reproductive healthcare awareness, accelerate diagnosis, and eliminate diagnostic ambiguity.

    The Clinical Problem

    Gynaecological disorders such as Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) and recurrent Urinary Tract Infections (UTI) suffer from chronic diagnostic delays—often taking months to years for confirmed diagnosis. Subjective reporting and symptom overlap frequently lead to misdiagnosis or late therapeutic intervention.

    The AI Solution

    GDDES introduces an evidence-based clinical decision support system utilizing a trained Random Forest Classifier achieving 99% accuracy across 16 critical symptom biomarkers. The model isolates distinct clusters for UTI, PCOS, and healthy physiological baselines in milliseconds.

    Review & Guidance Focus

    Beyond classification, GDDES translates predictions into actionable clinical understanding. Patients receive structured condition explanations, doctor care protocols, and verified real-world patient case reviews in clear English to understand their care pathway.
