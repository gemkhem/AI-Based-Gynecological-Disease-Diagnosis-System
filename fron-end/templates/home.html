    Rapid Diagnostic Reference

    PCOS Classification

    Polycystic Ovary Syndrome

    Characterized by ovulatory dysfunction, irregular or skipped menstrual periods, hyperandrogenic signs (acne, hirsutism), and metabolic weight gain.

    • Irregular or absent menses
    • Facial hair / androgenic hair growth
    • Hormonal acne & abdominal adiposity
    UTI Classification

    Urinary Tract Infection

    Bacterial infection of lower/upper urinary tract manifesting as intense burning upon micturition, increased frequency, and bladder/lumbar tenderness.

    • Urethral burning & dysuria
    • Urgent urine frequency
    • Lumbar / pelvic inflammation
    Healthy Classification

    Normal Physiological Baseline

    Symptom questionnaire confirms negative indicators for acute bacterial cystitis or endocrine ovulatory pathology.

    • Normal urinary flow and comfort
    • Regular menstrual rhythm
    • Absence of pelvic tenderness
