Diagnostic Scope

Targeted Clinical Classifications

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)

Hormonal and ovulatory imbalance associated with irregular menstruation, androgen excess, and cystic ovaries.

UTI (Urinary Tract Infection)

Bacterial inflammation of the urethra, bladder, or renal pelvis causing acute dysuria, frequency, and pelvic discomfort.

Healthy / Normal Flora

Absence of acute infectious or endocrine markers, verifying standard baseline gynaecological health.
